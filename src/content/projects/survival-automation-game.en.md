I'm developing an as-yet-unnamed **first-person survival and automation game** in **Unreal Engine**, primarily using **C++**. It is a solo project combining my interest in game development with the machinery, energy systems and technical problem solving I explore through mechanical engineering.

#### The idea

Players gather resources, build shelter and develop increasingly capable production systems. The aim is for automation to make a tangible difference to survival: each new tool or machine should reduce a familiar burden and open up new possibilities for exploration and building.

#### My work

As the sole designer and developer, I work on gameplay programming, systems architecture and project planning. The design brings together inventory, crafting, research, construction and production systems, with simulation supporting meaningful player decisions.

#### Current focus

I'm working towards a small, connected first playable build: gathering resources, crafting tools, unlocking and powering an early machine, and automating a previously manual task. Reliable save/load behaviour and clear feedback about recipes and machine states are part of that milestone.

The next step is to package the prototype for external playtesting, use feedback to improve the core loop, and establish a manageable demo scope before expanding the world and technology tree.
