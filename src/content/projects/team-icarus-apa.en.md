I have been a member of the **Structures team** on Team Icarus’s Advanced Propulsion Aircraft (APA) project at Politecnico di Torino since **October 2025**, and part of the Firmware team since **October 2026**.

#### My contribution

In the Structures team, I work with the team on structural design and analysis, using:

- **SolidWorks** for parametric 3D modelling and component design, with attention to manufacturing specifications.
- **HyperMesh** for model preparation, meshing and applying loads.
- **HyperView** for evaluating structural results and post-processing.

In the IT section, I help work on air telemetry and ait control systems, using:

- **C & Make** for the coding part, adhering to coding standards set by the team.
- **STM32 Microcontrollers** for running the code and interfaces. More specifically, we tend to experiment with NUCLEO G491RE boards, but are looking at other more powerful alternatives for our next versions of systems.
- **FreeRTOS & MavLink** as libraries for the STM32 MC.

This work brings together my mechanical engineering studies, my programming hobby and hands-on collaboration within a multidisciplinary aircraft team.
